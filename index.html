What is SubHub?

Nigeria's All-in-One
Digital Services Hub

SubHub is a Nigerian fintech platform designed to simplify everyday digital transactions. Whether you need to buy mobile data, top up airtime, pay your electricity bill, resell to customers or subscribe to cable TV. SubHub makes it all happen in seconds, from one app, at the best prices available anywhere in Nigeria.

We believe that every Nigerian deserves fast, reliable and affordable digital services — regardless of where they live or how tech-savvy they are. That's why SubHub is designed to be dead-simple, lightning fast and built specifically for the Nigerian experience.
