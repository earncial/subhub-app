How to Make Money Reselling Data in Nigeria (2026 Guide)

Data reselling has become one of the most accessible small businesses in Nigeria. With a smartphone, a SubHub account and as little as ₦5,000 in starting capital, anyone can start buying data at wholesale prices and reselling at a profit — from home, on campus or from a shop.

What is Data Reselling?

Data reselling means buying mobile internet data bundles at discounted VTU rates and selling them to end customers at market price, keeping the difference as profit. Platforms like SubHub allow you to buy 1GB of MTN data for ₦450 and sell it to your customers for ₦550–₦700 — earning ₦100–₦250 profit per transaction.

How Much Can You Earn Reselling Data in Nigeria?

Your earnings depend on how many transactions you do daily. Here's a realistic breakdown using MTN 1GB at ₦450 (SubHub price) sold at ₦600:

  • 10 sales/day = ₦1,500 daily profit → ₦45,000/month
  • 30 sales/day = ₦4,500 daily profit → ₦135,000/month
  • 50 sales/day = ₦7,500 daily profit → ₦225,000/month

The calculator above lets you model your own numbers. Most beginners start at 5–15 sales per day and grow from there as their customer base expands.

Which Network is Most Profitable to Resell?

MTN remains the highest-demand network in Nigeria due to its coverage and subscriber base, making it the easiest to sell. However, margins vary by network on SubHub:

  • MTN 1GB: ₦450 cost — high demand, easy to sell at ₦550–₦700
  • Airtel 1GB: ₦375 cost — popular in urban areas, good margins
  • Glo 1GB: ₦280 cost — lowest cost, popular in western Nigeria
  • 9Mobile 1GB: ₦250 cost — niche market, data sharing users

Many successful resellers sell across all four networks to serve more customers and maximize daily transactions.

How Much Capital Do You Need to Start?

You can start data reselling on SubHub with as little as ₦2,000–₦5,000 in your wallet. This covers roughly 10–20 transactions depending on the network and bundle size. As you collect payment from customers, reinvest into your SubHub wallet to keep the cycle going. Most resellers scale to ₦10,000–₦50,000 wallet balances within 2–4 weeks.

Where to Find Customers for Your Data Business

  • WhatsApp status and broadcast lists — free, effective, immediate
  • Your university or workplace — start with people you already know
  • Facebook groups in your area — local buy-and-sell groups
  • Referrals — give your first 5 customers a small discount to recommend you
  • Physical location — hostel, market stall, office — put up a simple sign
